About the experiment

THE ONE DOLLAR MYSTERY is a free community experiment. Participation does not require payment and does not offer financial returns or investments.

Participation notes

  • Participation is free.
  • The counter records anonymous app accounts, not verified people.
  • One anonymous account is intended to create one registration record.
  • Please do not submit personal or sensitive information.
PRIVACY POLICY

Information we collect

Firebase creates an anonymous account identifier when you join. A registration record and server-generated timestamp are stored. The website does not ask for your name, email, phone number, or payment details.

How information is used

The identifier and timestamp record participation and help display total registrations.

Service providers

This website uses Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore.

Payments

This version does not accept payments. Do not submit personal or sensitive information.

Data retention

Records may remain in the database while the experiment operates. This page does not currently provide an automated deletion request.
