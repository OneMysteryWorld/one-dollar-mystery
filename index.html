Participation notes

🔒 PRIVACY POLICY

What information is stored?

Firebase Anonymous Authentication creates an anonymous user ID. The database stores that ID and a join timestamp.

How is information used?

The anonymous ID helps prevent the same account from registering more than once.

Data and third-party services

This website uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore to provide registration and counter features.

Your choice

Participation is voluntary. You can choose not to join.
