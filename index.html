About the experiment

This is a free, voluntary global community experiment. Participation does not require payment, investment, or a promise of financial return.

Participation notes

  • Participation is free.
  • One registration per anonymous browser account.
  • The counter displays registration records and does not verify real-world identities.
  • No financial contribution is required or enabled in this version.
PRIVACY POLICY

What information is stored?

The site uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication to create an anonymous user ID. When you join, the database stores that ID as the document identifier and a join timestamp. The website does not ask for your name, email address, or phone number.

How is the information used?

The anonymous ID prevents the same browser account from registering more than once. The public counter displays the total number of registration records.

Data and third-party services

This website uses Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. Those services process technical information needed to provide the registration and counter features, according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Your choice

Participation is voluntary. You can choose not to join. This version does not provide a self-service deletion option.
